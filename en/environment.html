Let’s demand better!

Environment

We’re told we have to choose between the economy and the environment. That’s false. The NDP rejects the idea that a healthy environment and prosperity are at odds: green industries are a golden opportunity to create jobs.

  1. Commitment 1

    Invest massively in renewable energy

    Solar, wind, geothermal, energy efficiency: clean energy is the future, and it creates jobs here. Quebec, with its hydroelectricity, is well placed to benefit.

    What we propose

    We propose major investment in clean, renewable energy, funding research and development of new energy sources and green technologies, with incentives to adopt them.

    We need to share clean energy better between provinces, including through an east-west power grid where appropriate, and support renewable energy co-operatives. In infrastructure spending, public transit and home energy retrofits must come first.

  2. Commitment 2

    Not one more cent of public money for fossil fuels

    It makes no sense for public money to subsidize the industries driving the climate crisis. Those funds should go to the transition, while protecting the workers and communities affected.

    What we propose

    We propose to eliminate tax breaks and subsidies for fossil fuel industries, while protecting the workers and communities affected.

    We need binding greenhouse gas reduction targets, a carbon market that holds industry to them, and strict emissions and energy-efficiency standards for vehicles, appliances and buildings. And Canada must honour its international climate commitments.

  3. Commitment 3

    Protect our air and water

    Clean air and safe water mean less asthma, less illness and fewer disasters. Protecting the environment is a public health issue, today and for generations to come.

    What we propose

    We propose to enshrine in law the right of present and future generations to a healthy environment.

    Clean water is a right and a public resource, not a commodity: we need a national water strategy, protection from privatization and trade agreements, and a ban on bulk water exports. We also want to reduce and eliminate highly toxic substances, and strengthen the laws that protect biodiversity and species at risk.

Let’s demand better, together.
