Health and public services

Medicare was born of the NDP. As an epidemiologist, Nimâ knows that a strong public system protects everyone. We need to expand it, not sell it off to private interests.

  1. Commitment 1

    Strengthen dental care coverage

    Thanks to the NDP, millions of people now have access to dental care. We need to build on those gains and make dental care a permanent part of our public health system.

    What we propose

    We propose making dental care a permanent part of our public health system, like every other medically necessary service.

    To get there, we need to increase health transfers to the provinces and guarantee fully public services, free of financial barriers.

  2. Commitment 2

    Cover all care, from head to toe

    Eyes, ears, teeth, mental health, medication: it’s all health care. No one should go without care because they can’t afford it.

    What we propose

    We propose expanding the public system to cover dental, mental wellness, pharmaceutical, hearing and vision care. Mental health and addiction services must be accessible to everyone, as must home and palliative care.

    We need national standards for long-term care, including a minimum of four hours of care per day. We also need to recruit and train more doctors, nurses and health professionals, and create a Crown corporation to make vaccines, medicines and medical equipment here.

  3. Commitment 3

    Stop the privatization of our public services

    Every dollar that goes to private profit is a dollar less for care. Our public services must stay public: funded by all, accessible to all.

    What we propose

    We propose to fight the privatization of health care and require the federal government to enforce the Canada Health Act. We must take profit out of long-term care and build more public facilities.

    More broadly, we oppose costly and inefficient public-private partnerships, and every form of privatization of our public services, which should be delivered by public sector workers.

Let’s demand better, together.
